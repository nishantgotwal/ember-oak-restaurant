# Obsidian Reserve

Create an ultra-luxury, modern fine-dining & artisanal restaurant web application with a dark obsidian luxury aesthetic (#0B0B0C background, #161618 cards, frosted glass accents with backdrop-blur, metallic gold & warm champagne highlights #D4AF37 / #E5C07B).

Key requirements:
1. Typography & Visual Identity:
   - Editorial serif headlines (Cinzel / Playfair Display style) with character tracking and subtle gradients.
   - All-caps tracked geometric sans-serif for badges and subtitles.
   - Elegant handwritten script accents for chef's special notes.
   - Micro-interactions, smooth hover effects, golden border highlights, and scroll animations.

2. Front Page / Hero Section:
   - Full-width looping cinematic background video showing sizzling open-flame cooking, slow-motion plating of dishes, and wine pouring, overlaid with a subtle dark vignette gradient for readability.
   - Evocative headline: "Artisanal Gastronomy Meets Woodfire Mastery" with Michelin-style recommendation badge ("Voted Best Dining Experience 2026").
   - Primary CTA: "Reserve a Table" (opens reservation modal).
   - Secondary CTA: "Explore Menu" (scrolls to interactive menu).
   - Floating quick-info bar with live opening hours status, today's tasting menu highlight, and contact/directions.

3. Interactive Menu & Ordering:
   - Category navigation tabs: Starters, Signature Mains, Woodfire Grill, Artisan Desserts, Craft Cocktails & Wine Cellar.
   - Dietary & preference filters: Chef's Special, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Spice Level.
   - High-definition dish cards with hover zoom, tasting notes, allergens, pricing, and "Add to Order / Bag" button.
   - Slide-over cart/takeaway drawer with item customizations, tip selector, and order summary.

4. Table Reservation Engine:
   - Multi-step booking modal: Guest count (1 to 10+), Date & Time slot, Seating preference (Main Dining Room, Chef's Counter, Outdoor Patio, Private Dining Room), contact details & special occasion notes.
   - Instant confirmation view with reservation summary.

5. Chef's Story & Philosophy:
   - Bento grid showcasing the Executive Chef, farm-to-table sourcing, and kitchen behind-the-scenes gallery.

6. Signature Dishes Showcase:
   - Interactive 3D tilt/carousel slider of top signature creations with pairing recommendations and flavor profiles.

7. Social Proof, Location & Footer:
   - Press quotes, guest review cards, and interactive Instagram-style food gallery.
   - Location, interactive map style, valet info, dress code guide, and private event inquiry form.
   - VIP Club newsletter signup and luxury footer.
Responsive across desktop and mobile.
